## About

23N AI Labs is an independent research group working on applied AI — evaluation, forecasting, small language models, and agentic systems — with a bias toward **work you can check**. Every public study ships its code, its data (synthetic or clearly licensed), and the evidence behind its claims, so results can be reproduced rather than taken on trust.

We publish under three principles:

- **Reproducible** — pinned releases, deterministic builds, and a reproduction package with each study.
- **Open & privacy-safe** — synthetic or properly-licensed data only; no private records, no scraped third-party content.
- **Honestly attributed** — real authors only, AI assistance disclosed, and published versions preserved (new findings get a new version).
