### `// THE LAB`

**23N Labs** explores the intersection of foundational AI research, rigorous evaluation, and production-minded engineering. We are interested in systems that are **efficient, reliable, adaptable, and useful**—not merely larger.

Our work combines experimental research with a bias toward reproducible evidence and practical deployment.

### `// SELECTED RESEARCH`

#### The Diwali Trap · Mithai Shop Challenge

**Can forecasting models handle the shifting timing of a major festival—and help a small business plan?**

A reproducible, **synthetic** demand-forecasting and planning experiment exploring calendar shifts and operational decision-making. The public release includes a technical appendix, code and notebooks, figures, a claim ledger, and provenance disclosures. It does **not** represent real retailer transaction data or independently verified operational savings.

[Read the research](https://23nlabs.com/research/mithai-shop-challenge) · [Explore repository](https://github.com/23nlabs/mithai-shop-challenge) · [Reproduce the work](https://github.com/23nlabs/mithai-shop-challenge/blob/main/REPRODUCIBILITY.md)

### `// HOW WE WORK`

```text
QUESTION  →  HYPOTHESIS  →  EXPERIMENT  →  EVALUATION  →  REPRODUCE  →  PUBLISH
```

- **Evidence over hype.** Clear methods, baselines, and limitations.
- **Reproducibility by design.** Artifacts and instructions wherever possible.
- **Engineering rigor.** Real-world constraints are part of the problem.
- **Open collaboration.** Useful technical dialogue and constructive critique.
